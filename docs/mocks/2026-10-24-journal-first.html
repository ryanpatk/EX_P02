B · A space is a working board

Open a space and the Journal narrows to a side column scoped to that space, so you can keep jotting while you work. The board is what got filed there: pinned sites, open tasks, notes and a gallery of saves. Drag anything from the Journal onto the board to file it.

C · The Library: a visual database behind the Journal

Everything you've ever captured, as one database. Gallery (today's masonry), Table and Board are three views of the same records, and every view can be saved. Source, space and type are columns, so “everything I bookmarked on X this month and never filed” is just a filter.

D · Mobile (React Native): Journal first, capture from anywhere

The phone is mostly a Journal: open it, scroll, jot with your thumb. The iOS/Android share sheet drops anything into the same feed, with an optional space. The Library is the second tab, and Spaces sit inside it.
